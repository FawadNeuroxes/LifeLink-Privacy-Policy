How It Works

From request to donation in three steps

Built for emergencies: every step is verified, and every step happens inside the app.

01

Raise a verified request

Upload the hospital requisition and verify the attendant's phone number. Only verified requests go live, so donors are never asked for something that isn't real.

02

Nearby donors are alerted

Eligible donors within 7, then 15, then 40 km are alerted in waves, ranked by distance and reliability. Donors in a cooldown or deferral are skipped automatically.

03

Donate, and the bank confirms

The donor goes to the blood bank, which confirms the donation with a one-time code. The donor's history and next-eligible date update on their own.

Who It's For

One network, three roles

Pick your role when you join. You can be a donor and a requester at the same time.

Donors

Get alerted when someone near you needs your blood group. Accept or decline, chat in the app, track your donations and see when you can donate next.

Patients' families

Raise a request for a patient in minutes, follow who has committed, and coordinate with donors without sharing phone numbers.

Blood banks

Register your bank, get verified, confirm donations with a one-time code, defer donors when needed and reserve stock for requests.

Why LifeLink

Built to be fast, safe, and private

Privacy by design

Other users never see your phone number, date of birth or health answers — only a donor code, blood group, availability and a location rounded to about 1 km.

Smart eligibility

Donors in cooldown, deferred, underweight or outside the 18–65 age range are never alerted, and a clinician at the bank always makes the final call.

Verified blood banks

Only banks approved by LifeLink can confirm donations, record a bank-tested blood group and reserve stock for a request.

Pledged circles

For recurring transfusions, a circle of consenting donors takes turns, with reminders and automatic hand-over if someone can't make it.

Alerts that respect you

Set quiet hours and an alert limit. Non-critical alerts wait until morning; critical ones still reach you, and everything lands in your in-app inbox.

Safety tools, in your control

Chat inside the app, block or report anyone, and delete your account and personal data from Settings whenever you like.
