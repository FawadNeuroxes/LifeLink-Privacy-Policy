Introduction

Welcome to LifeLink ("we," "our," or "us"). LifeLink is an emergency blood network that connects people who need blood with nearby volunteer donors and registered blood banks. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our mobile application (the "App"), how we use and protect it, and the choices you have.

Important: LifeLink handles health-related information such as your blood group and donation history. We collect only what the service needs, we never sell it, and we never show ads. By using the App you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Information We Collect

1. Information you give us

  • Account: your mobile phone number, used to sign in. Verification codes are sent by SMS through Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile: your name, city, date of birth, gender and weight band, and your chosen role (donor, requester, or blood bank).
  • Donor information: your blood group (self-declared until a blood bank verifies it), your answers to the health-screening questions, your availability, and settings such as quiet hours and alert limits.
  • Blood requests: the request details (blood group, units and component needed, urgency and hospital), the hospital requisition photo or document you upload, and the attendant's phone number, which is verified with a one-time code.
  • Pledged circles: if you set up a circle for recurring transfusions, the patient's name and transfusion schedule that you enter as guardian, and the donors you invite.
  • Messages: chats between a requester and a donor inside a request.
  • Blood bank registration: the bank's name, licence number, address, opening hours, contact person and phone number, and an optional photo.
  • Reports: anything you submit when you report a user or content, and your private block list.
  • Profile photo: an optional avatar you upload.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Location: your approximate and precise location, only with your permission and only while you use the App. It is used to find donors near a hospital and to show you nearby requests. Other users only ever see it rounded to roughly 1 km.
  • Donation records: donations confirmed by a blood bank and the dates when you can next donate.
  • Reliability data: a score that rises when you complete donations and falls when you don't show up for a request you committed to.
  • Push-notification token: a device identifier from Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can alert you.
  • Device and diagnostic data: crash reports and performance data from Firebase Crashlytics (such as device model, operating system version and app version), plus device-integrity signals from Firebase App Check that help us block abuse.
  • Usage analytics: screen views and a small number of product events through Firebase Analytics, tied only to a random Firebase user ID. We do not send your phone number, name, blood group, location or any free text to analytics. Analytics and crash reporting are switched off in development builds.

3. Financial information

  • LifeLink is free. We do not collect, process or store any payment or card information, and the App does not support payments between users.

What Other People Can See

Your phone number, date of birth and health answers are never shown to other users.

  • Other users see only a donor code (for example #482), blood group, availability, reliability score, and a location rounded to roughly 1 km.
  • Requesters and donors chat inside the App, so no one has to share a phone number.
  • The blood bank named on a request can see that request's requisition photo and the attendant's phone number.
  • The blood bank where you donate can see the details needed to confirm your donation and may record a verified blood group or a deferral.
  • LifeLink administrators can review blood bank applications and user reports in order to keep the service safe.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Match and alert: find eligible donors near a hospital and send you alerts.
  • Confirm donations: issue and redeem one-time donation codes, record donations and work out when you can next donate.
  • Issue certificates: create donation certificates for you.
  • Coordinate: manage pledged donor circles and in-app chat.
  • Keep the service safe: verify requests, prevent abuse and spam, and act on reports.
  • Improve and maintain the App: fix crashes, measure performance and understand which features are used.
  • Meet legal obligations where they apply.

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and the App shows no ads.

Health Information

Your health-screening answers, weight band and any deferral recorded by a blood bank are visible only to you and are used only to decide whether you are eligible to donate.

  • If a clinician defers you, we tell you only that a medical assessment prevented donating. The reason is kept on your private donor profile and is never put in a notification.
  • A blood group you enter yourself is marked unverified. A blood group tested by a verified blood bank is marked verified.
  • LifeLink does not give medical advice. A clinician at the blood bank always makes the final decision about whether you can donate.

App Permissions

The App asks for the following Android permissions. You can change them at any time in your device settings.

  • Location (approximate and precise): to match you with nearby requests and donors. If you turn it off, you can still use the App but matching is limited. We do not use background location.
  • Camera: to scan a donation QR code and to take a photo of a hospital requisition or your profile picture. Photos can also be chosen from your gallery through the system picker.
  • Notifications: to alert you about requests, donation confirmations and circle reminders.
  • Internet access, vibration and start-up: to talk to our servers, vibrate for alerts, and restore alerts after your device restarts.

Service Providers

LifeLink runs on Google Firebase, part of Google Cloud. These providers process data on our behalf to run the service:

Firebase Authentication

  • Purpose: phone sign-in and one-time verification codes (SMS).
  • Data: your phone number.

Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage and Cloud Functions

  • Purpose: storing your account and donor data, requests, chats and uploaded photos, and running the server-side logic for matching, donation codes and account deletion.
  • Data: the information described in this policy. Uploaded requisition photos can be viewed only by the requester and the staff of the named blood bank.

Firebase Cloud Messaging

  • Purpose: delivering push notifications.
  • Data: a device token and notification content.

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

  • Purpose: crash reporting and understanding how the App is used.
  • Data: device and diagnostic data and coarse product events, linked only to a random Firebase user ID.

Firebase App Check

  • Purpose: confirming that requests come from a genuine copy of the App, to reduce abuse.
  • Data: device-integrity signals.

These services are governed by Google's own privacy terms, including the Firebase privacy information and Google Privacy Policy.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • No data sales: we do not sell, trade or rent your personal information.
  • With other users and blood banks only as described in “What Other People Can See”.
  • With service providers listed above, only as needed to run the App.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, the public or LifeLink.

International Data Transfers

Our server-side functions run in Google Cloud's Mumbai (asia-south1) region, and Google may process or store data in other countries where it operates. By using the App you understand that your information may be processed outside your country. These transfers are covered by Google's security measures and contractual protections.

Your Choices and Rights

Controls inside the App

  • Edit your profile and donor details at any time in Profile and Settings.
  • Turn location off, set quiet hours and an alert limit, or mark yourself unavailable.
  • Block users and report content or people.
  • Withdraw from a request you committed to, or leave a pledged circle.
  • Delete your account and personal data in Settings → Delete account.

Requests to us

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or to object to how we use it. Email us at support@neuroxes.com and we will respond. We may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder first.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • While your account is active: we keep your profile, donor profile and activity so the service works.
  • Chats and notifications: kept with your account and erased when you delete it.
  • Requests: requests expire automatically if they are not completed.
  • Analytics and diagnostic data: kept for the period set in our Firebase project settings, linked only to a random ID.
  • Local data: files you save or share from the App remain on your device until you delete them.

When you delete your account

You can delete your account in Settings → Delete account, after verifying your phone with a fresh code. Deletion erases your profile, donor profile, notifications, block list, chats, pending donation codes, requisition photos and avatar, removes your sign-in record, and cancels your open requests. See Delete Account for the full list.

What we keep: without your personal details, we keep the donation records that blood banks rely on, closed requests, and safety reports and audit entries. These can no longer be linked to you by name or phone number.

Children's Privacy

LifeLink is not directed at children. You must be at least 18 to register as a donor, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Data Security

We use technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in transit: data sent between the App and our servers is encrypted.
  • Server-side control: requests, donations, matching and other sensitive records can be written only by our server functions, not directly by apps. Security rules limit who can read each record.
  • Private by default: user profiles and donor profiles are private; other users see only a separate, minimal directory entry with a rounded location.
  • Access limits: administrator tools are restricted to approved staff.
  • Abuse protection: App Check and rate limits reduce automated abuse.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes we will:

  • Update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this policy.
  • Notify you in the App and, where required, ask you to accept the updated policy.

Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
