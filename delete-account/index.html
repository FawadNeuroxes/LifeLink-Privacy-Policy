Delete Your Account & Data

You can permanently delete your LifeLink account and personal data yourself, from inside the App. It is verified, irreversible, and takes less than a minute.

Applies to: LifeLink accounts (Android) Verification: one-time code sent to your phone

How to delete your account

  1. 1

    Open Settings

    In LifeLink, go to Profile → Settings and choose Delete account.

  2. 2

    Verify your phone

    Tap to send a one-time code to the phone number on your account and enter it. Android may fill the code in for you.

  3. 3

    Confirm

    Tick the box to confirm you understand this is permanent, then delete. Your data is erased and you are signed out.

What will be deleted

  • Your profile (name, city, date of birth, gender, weight band)
  • Your donor profile, health answers, availability and directory entry
  • Your notifications, block list and chats
  • Pending donation codes
  • Requisition photos you uploaded and your profile photo
  • Your sign-in record, including your phone number
  • Your open requests are cancelled, and your commitments and circle seats are released

What we keep

  • Donation records that blood banks rely on, kept without your personal details
  • Closed requests, kept without personal details
  • Safety reports and audit entries, kept without personal details
  • Analytics and crash data linked only to a random ID, until Firebase's retention period ends
  • Files you saved or shared from the App on your own device (delete these yourself)

This can't be undone. Once your account is deleted we cannot recover your profile, donation history, chats or certificates. If you are the only manager of a blood bank, deletion is refused until another manager is added.

Can't open the App?

If you've lost access to your phone number or can't use the App, email us from an address you can be reached on and ask for your account to be deleted. We will ask you to confirm that the account is yours before we delete anything, and we'll reply to confirm once it's done.

Email support@neuroxes.com

Please don't include passwords or verification codes in your email.
